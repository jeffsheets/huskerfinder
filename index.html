What is the flagship radio station for Nebraska Huskers?

The Huskers Radio Network has several flagship stations. KLIN 1400 AM in Lincoln is a major flagship, and KRVN 880 AM in Lexington is the powerful rural Nebraska flagship with 50,000 watts covering much of the Great Plains. KFAB 1110 AM in Omaha was historically a flagship station.

How does the Husker Radio Finder work?

It uses your device's GPS to determine your location, then calculates the distance to actual FCC radio tower locations for each station in the network. Sort by distance or by what should sound best — signal estimates use the FCC's own propagation curves with each station's licensed power and antenna height, account for AM stations' mandatory nighttime power cuts, and favor FM over AM at similar strength for its clearer audio (how it works). Your location stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

What station is the Husker game on inside Memorial Stadium?

Inside Memorial Stadium in Lincoln, the Husker Radio Network feed is available on 87.7 FM so fans in the stands can listen to the live broadcast during the game.
